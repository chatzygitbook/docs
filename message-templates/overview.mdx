The **Message Templates** page is your central hub for creating and managing all your rich-media and structured messages for different channels. This allows you to design engaging content that your chatbot can use for proactive communication, promotional campaigns, or transactional messages.
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/overview.png" alt="ig1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
From this page, you can select a channel to begin creating templates:

* **WhatsApp**: Create pre-approved templates that follow WhatsApp's strict guidelines, such as Marketing, Utility, or Authentication messages.
* **Facebook Messenger**: Design rich templates with various categories like Text, Image, Buttons, and Quick Replies for your Facebook chatbot.
* **Instagram Messaging**: Build engaging templates for Instagram, including options for Text, Images, Buttons, and Quick Replies, to communicate with your followers.
