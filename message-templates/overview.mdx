The **Message Templates** page is your central hub for creating and managing reusable message templates across supported communication channels. These templates help you automate conversations, maintain consistent messaging, and streamline customer interactions.

<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/overview.png" alt="Message Templates home, with a card for each messaging channel" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

From this page, choose the channel for which you want to create and manage templates:

- **WhatsApp** – Create Meta-approved message templates for Marketing, Utility, Authentication, and Call Permission requests. These templates can be used in AI Agents, Journeys, and WhatsApp campaigns.

- **Facebook Messenger** – Build reusable templates with rich content such as text, images, buttons, quick replies, and other interactive elements for Messenger conversations.

- **Instagram** – Create templates for Instagram Direct Messages, story replies, and comment replies to automate customer engagement and lead generation.

- **LinkedIn** – Create reusable text message templates for LinkedIn outreach, including personalized messages and connection requests that can be used within Journeys and automation workflows.

> **Tip:** Templates can include dynamic variables, allowing you to personalize messages with customer information such as names, order details, appointment times, and other runtime values.
