The **WhatsApp Message Templates** section is where you manage your pre-approved messages for outbound communication on WhatsApp. These templates are required by WhatsApp for all messages sent outside of an active conversation window.

To create a new template, click the **`+ Create Template`** button. Once a template is submitted, WhatsApp can take up to **24 hours** to review it.  
In some cases, approval may happen within a minute, but a pending status implies a manual review is needed.

### Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides an overview of all your templates:

| Column               | Description                                                                 |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Template ID**       | A unique identifier for the template.                                       |
| **Template Name**     | The name you assigned to the template.                                      |
| **Template Language** | The language of the template's content (e.g., English).                     |
| **Template Category** | The purpose of the template (e.g., Marketing, Utility, Authentication).      |
| **Template Status**   | The approval status from WhatsApp (`Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Pending`). |

### Creating WhatsApp Message Templates

The creation process varies slightly depending on the template’s category, but all templates must be **submitted to WhatsApp for approval** before they can be used for outbound communication.

---

#### Template Categories

When creating a template, you must select a **category**, which defines its purpose and allowed content:

- **Marketing** → Promotional content, product announcements, special offers.  
- **Utility** → Transactional updates, order status, delivery notifications, appointment reminders.  
- **Authentication** → Secure verification messages with one-time passcodes (OTPs) for account logins or sign-ups.

#### Marketing & Utility Templates

Marketing and Utility templates share the same creation interface. They support rich content and personalization:

- **Template Name** → Unique identifier for your template.  
- **Template Language** → Language of the message.  
- **Header** *(optional)* → Can include text, an image, or a document.  
- **Body** → Main content of the message. Supports variables (`{{1}}`, `{{2}}`) for personalization.  
- **Footer** *(optional)* → Smaller text section at the bottom of the message.  
- **Buttons** → Up to 10 interactive buttons for customer responses or actions.

#### Authentication Templates

Authentication templates are more restricted due to security requirements. The form is pre-filled and cannot be freely edited. You may only configure limited options:

- **Add Security Recommendation** → Includes a disclaimer like: *“For your security, do not share this code.”*  
- **Add Expiry Time** → Adds an expiration notice and lets you specify how long the code remains valid.
