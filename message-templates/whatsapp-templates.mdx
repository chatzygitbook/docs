## How to Create WhatsApp Template Messages in Chatzy AI

Hey there! Welcome to this guide, where you will learn how to create WhatsApp template messages in **Chatzy AI**.

Before moving forward, make sure you have connected your **WhatsApp number** to Chatzy AI and it is visible under **Channels → WhatsApp**.

If not, please check the previous tutorial video before proceeding.

---

### Step 1: Navigate to Message Templates

- Go to the **Message Templates** section of Chatzy AI.  
- Select the **WhatsApp** channel to view your existing templates and their approval status.  
- If you haven’t created any templates yet, this page will be empty.  
- Click on the **`+ Create Template`** button to start creating a new template.

---

### Step 2: Select the Right Category

WhatsApp requires you to choose a **category** for each template. This defines its purpose:

| Category        | Purpose |
|------------------|--------|
| **Authentication** | One-time passwords (OTPs), login verification. |
| **Utility**       | Transactional updates: order confirmations, account updates, reminders. |
| **Marketing**     | Promotional content, sales announcements, brand awareness. |

> 💡 **Tip:** Choosing the correct category is essential. Selecting the wrong one may lead to rejection by Meta.  
> Marketing templates usually cost more to send. For pricing details, visit the **Pricing** page on Chatzy AI.

### Step 3: Add Basic Information

- **Template Name** → Pick a unique and easy-to-identify name.  
- **Language** → Choose the language of your message.  
- Decide if the template will contain **text only** or **media**.

If adding **media** (image, video, or document):  
- Select the media type in the **Header** dropdown and upload your file.  
- The uploaded media is only for preview. You will need to upload it again while sending campaigns.

You can also add **text in the header**. Header text appears in **bold** at the top.

---

### Step 4: Write the Message

- **Body** → This is the main message content. Supports emojis, **bold**, and *italic* text.  
- Keep it **short and engaging** for better delivery rates.

- **Footer** *(optional)* → Use it for greetings or less prominent info.

---

### Step 5: Add Interactivity with Buttons

Buttons make your template **interactive**:

| Button Type         | Action |
|----------------------|-------|
| **Quick Reply**      | Lets users reply with predefined options directly in WhatsApp. |
| **Call to Action (CTA)** | Takes users to a webpage or initiates a phone call. |

### Step 6: Use Variables for Personalization

Variables let you insert dynamic, user-specific details like **names, order numbers, or dates**.

- Add variables in the **Header** (Text type) or **Body** using **double curly brackets**.  
- Example: `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` for multiple placeholders.  
- Add **sample values** for each variable to help Meta understand and approve your template faster.

> ⚠️ These sample values are for demonstration only. You must provide real values when sending campaigns.

---

### Step 7: Review & Submit

Once everything looks good in the preview:

- Click **Create** to submit your template.  
- Meta usually approves templates within a few seconds, but it may take up to **24 hours**.  
- Refresh the page to check the template’s status.

## ✅ Final Tips
- Follow WhatsApp’s **template guidelines** strictly.  
- Submitting **promotional language** under **Utility** or **Authentication** categories may cause **rejection**.
