The **Instagram Message Templates** section is where you can design and manage rich, structured messages for your Instagram Business chatbot.  
These templates let you send engaging content, showcase products, and guide conversations directly with your followers and customers.

## Template Categories
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig2.png" alt="ig2" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

When creating a new template, you must select a **category**, which determines its content and format:

- **TEXT** → Send simple, plain-text messages to users for quick communication.  
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig3.png" alt="ig3" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

- **IMAGE**, **VIDEO**, **AUDIO**, **FILE** → Send rich media content to make your conversations more engaging.  
  - Perfect for visual storytelling, product showcases, or promotional content.  
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig4.png" alt="ig4" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

- **BUTTON** → Create interactive messages with a clickable button that directs users to a website, landing page, or product link.  
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig5.png" alt="ig5" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

- **QUICK_REPLY** → Provide users with a set of predefined quick reply options.  
  This guides the conversation, helps capture user intent, and speeds up responses.  
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig6.png" alt="ig6" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

- **GENERIC** → Build rich, interactive cards that combine a **title, subtitle, image, and buttons** in one structured template.  
  Widely used for displaying **product catalogs, service menus, or promotional offers** in a visually appealing way.  
<Frame>
  <img src="/message-templates/instagram-templates/ig7.png" alt="ig7" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

---

> 💡 **Tip:** Use **Generic Templates** when you need to showcase multiple products or offers in a **carousel format**, letting users swipe through items and take instant action.
