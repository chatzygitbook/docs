### Campaign Statistics

The **Stats** tab provides a detailed breakdown of your campaign's performance after it has been sent. Here, you can track key metrics and monitor the delivery status of each message.

#### WhatsApp Message Statistics

This section shows real-time analytics for your campaign messages:

* **Sent**: The total number of messages that have been sent to recipients.  
* **Delivered**: The number of messages that were successfully delivered to the recipients' devices.  
* **Read**: The number of messages that have been read by the recipients.  
* **Failed**: The number of messages that failed to send or be delivered.   
* **Save Failed Contacts**: You also have the option to save a list of contacts whose messages failed, making it easier to retry or follow up later.  


You can click the **Refresh** button in the top right corner of this section to get the latest, most up-to-date statistics for your campaign.

#### Campaign Runs

This table provides a log of each individual message sent as part of the campaign:

* **Contact**: The contact who received the message.  
* **State**: The current status of the message, such as `Sent`, `Delivered`, `Read`, or `Failed`.  
* **Created At**: The timestamp when the message was sent.
