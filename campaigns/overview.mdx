The **Campaigns** section is where you manage your proactive, outbound messaging. Unlike journeys, which are triggered by a user's action, campaigns can be scheduled or sent to a specific group of recipients to drive engagement, announce a new product, or send a promotion.


<Frame>
  <img src="/campaigns/overview.png" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
The dashboard provides a quick overview of all your campaigns:

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | The custom name you gave the campaign. |
| **Channel** | The messaging platform the campaign is deployed on (e.g., WhatsApp). |
| **Recipient Type** | The type of audience the campaign is targeted at, such as leads or a specific contact list. |
| **Campaign Triggered** | The event that starts the campaign, whether it's a specific date, time, or another trigger. |
| **Created At** | The date and time the campaign was created. |

To create a new campaign, click the **`+ Create Campaign`** button in the top right corner.
