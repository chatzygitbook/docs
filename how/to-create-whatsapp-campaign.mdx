A WhatsApp campaign allows you to send the **same message to many contacts at once**.  
It is commonly used for:  
- Promotions  
- Announcements  
- Event reminders  
- Bulk updates  

---

### 1. Before You Begin

Ensure the following prerequisites are completed:  
1. WhatsApp channel is connected  
2. An approved WhatsApp message template is available  
3. A contact list with target recipients is created  

---

### 2. Navigate to Campaigns

1. Go to the **left sidebar**  
2. Click **Campaigns**  
3. The Campaigns page displays a table of existing campaigns with:  
   - Campaign Name  
   - Trigger Date  
   - Channel  
   - Target List  

---

### 3. Create a New Campaign

1. Click **Create Campaign**  
2. Enter a unique and recognizable **Campaign Name**  

---

### 4. Set Channel and Recipients

1. Select **Channel: WhatsApp**  
2. Choose **Recipient Type: List**  
3. Select the target **Contact List** from your existing lists  

---

### 5. Choose Message Type

1. Select **Message Type: WhatsApp Template**  
2. Choose from your list of **approved templates**  

---

### 6. Fill in Template Parameters

If the selected template contains placeholders (parameters), configure them as follows:  

- **Text Parameters**  
  - Enter values manually, or  
  - Use contact variables such as `{{ contact.firstname }}`  
  - Browse available variables using the variable selector  

- **Media Parameters (if applicable)**  
  - Upload the required media file (image, video, document)  
  - Use the **Upload Resource** option  

*Example:* Personalize your campaign with `{{ contact.firstname }}`, which auto-fills each recipient’s first name.  

---

### 7. Test Your Campaign

1. Click **Create and Test**  
2. Review your message in the **preview window**  
3. Select a contact (e.g., yourself) for testing  
4. Click **Send Test Message** to validate the campaign output  

---

### 8. Launch Your Campaign

After testing is successful:  
- Click **Send Campaign**  
- Choose one of the following options:  
  - **Send Now** – Launch immediately  
  - **Schedule for Later** – Define a date and time for future delivery  

---

### 9. Monitor Campaign Performance

Once the campaign is active, monitor:  
- Number of contacts messaged  
- Delivery success rate  
- Read receipts  
- Failed attempts  

*Failures may result from invalid numbers, opt-outs, or delivery restrictions.*  

---

### 10. Summary

You have successfully created and sent a WhatsApp campaign using Chatzy AI.  
Campaigns enable you to broadcast promotions, updates, and reminders to your audience at scale.
