This guide explains how to enable and setup WhatsApp calling to assign a Voice AI Agent to answer incoming calls or make outgoing calls via Chatzy AI.

---

### Requirements

- **Connected WhatsApp Number:** You must have a WhatsApp number connected in the [**Channels**](https://app.chatzy.ai/channels/whatsapp) section.
- **Voice AI Agent:** You must have a configured Voice AI Agent.

---

### Step 1: Enable WhatsApp Calling

1. Navigate to **Channels** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click on **WhatsApp**. Your connected WhatsApp numbers will appear here.
3. Click the **Three Dots** icon under the **Actions** column and select **Enable WhatsApp Calling**. The configuration menu will appear.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall1.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
4. Check the box labeled **Enable WhatsApp calling**.
5. You may set up weekly working hours according to your needs. **Note:** Don't forget to select the appropriate **Time Zone**.
6. Once configured, click **Save** or **Update** to apply changes.

---

### Step 2: Set Up the Call Permission Template

1. Navigate to the **Message Templates** section of Chatzy AI.
2. Select the **WhatsApp** channel to view your existing templates and their status.
3. Click the `+ Create Template` button to begin.
4. Set the **Category** to **Utility**, enter an appropriate name for the template, and select the **Language**.
5. In the **Body** field, write the message you want to display when asking the user for call permission.
6. Check the box labeled **Call permission request**.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall2.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
7. You can review a preview of the template on the right side of the screen.
8. Once satisfied, click **Create**.

> WhatsApp can take up to 24 hours to review (approve/reject) a template.

---
### Step 3: Set Up Voice AI Agent for Incoming Calls

1. Head over to the **Journeys** section of Chatzy AI.
2. Click **Create Journey**. Enter an appropriate name, select the **Blank Template**, and click **Create**.
3. Click **Add Trigger** and select **Incoming Call**.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall3.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
4. Add a new action and select **Assign to Voice AI Agent**.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall4.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
5. Open the settings for the **Assign to Voice AI Agent** action. Click the dropdown menu labeled **Select Voice AI Agent** and choose the specific agent you wish to use.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall5.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
6. Once finished, click **Save** and then **Publish** to activate the journey.

---
### Step 4: Set Up Voice AI Agent for Outbound Calls

1. Head over to the **Journeys** section of Chatzy AI.
2. Click **Create Journey**. Enter an appropriate name, select the **Blank Template**, and click **Create**.
3. Click **Add Trigger** and select **Inbound Webhook**, then click **Create Webhook**.
4. Configure your external application to send a `POST` request to the generated URL.
    * *Example:* Sending appointment booking details to the webhook.
5. Refresh the webhook to view incoming data and map the fields (e.g., `name`, `phone_number`).
    > **Note:** Remember to include the `phone_number` in your payload.
6. Set the **Contact Identifier** value to the phone number field.
    > `{{inbound_webhook.phone_number}}`

<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall6.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

7. Add a new action and select **Initiate Outbound WhatsApp Call**.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall8.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
8. Open the settings for the **Initiate Outbound WhatsApp Call** action. In the **Phone Number** field, insert the same contact identifier variable: `{{inbound_webhook.phone_number}}`.
9. Click the dropdown menu labeled **Select Voice AI Agent** and choose the specific agent you wish to use.
<Frame>
  <img src="/how/wacall7.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
10. Once finished, click **Save** and then **Publish** to activate the journey.
