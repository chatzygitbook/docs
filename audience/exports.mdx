The **Exports** section shows all data exports made from your account.
<Frame>
  <img src="/audience/exports.png" />
</Frame>
##  What You Can See:

* Export Name
* Status (in progress / completed / Failed)
* Type (Contacts, Lists)
* Created At timestamp

##  When Exports Are Triggered:


* You manually export data from the Contacts, Lists sections
