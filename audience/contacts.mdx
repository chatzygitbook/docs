### Contacts Dashboard

The **Contacts Dashboard** is your central hub for managing all your leads and customers. Here, you can view, filter, and organize your contact list to better manage your audience.
<Frame>
  <img src="/audience/contacts.png" />
</Frame>
---

#### Key Features

* **Search & Filters**: Quickly find contacts using the search bar or filter them by selecting a specific **list** or **tags**. This allows you to segment your audience for targeted campaigns.  
* **Import CSV**: Import contacts in bulk from an existing file to quickly add your entire contact list.  
* **Create**: Use the **`+ Create`** button to manually add a new contact.  
* **Contact List**: The main table provides a clear overview of your contacts, including their name, email, phone number, and when their record was created or updated.  

---

### Contact Management

The dashboard makes it easy to manage your audience by either manually creating contacts or importing them in bulk.

---

#### Create New Contact

When you click the **`+ Create`** button, a pop-up appears where you can manually add a single contact.
<Frame>
  <img src="/audience/n-c.png" />
</Frame>
* **First Name & Last Name**: Enter the contact's name.  
* **Email & Phone Number**: Used as primary identifiers. At least one is required.  
* **Blacklisted**: Prevents any communication from being sent to this contact.  
* **Frozen**: Temporarily pauses communication with the contact.  
* **Tags**: Add labels to categorize the contact (e.g., `VIP`, `Lead`, `Support`).  
* **Attributes**: Store custom data points (e.g., `City`, `Last Purchase Date`).  

---

#### Import Contacts (CSV)

The **Import CSV** option lets you upload a spreadsheet to add multiple contacts at once. The process has three steps:
<Frame>
  <img src="/audience/i-c.png" />
</Frame>
**1. Upload CSV File**  
* The file must include either a `phone_number` or an `email` column.  
* Accepted column headers: `phone_number`, `email`, `full_name`.  
* Maximum of **10,000 contacts** per upload.  
* Phone numbers must include the country code and contain no spaces or special characters.  

**2. Review Sample**  
* After uploading, you can review a sample of your data to confirm proper formatting.  

**3. Apply Categories**  
* Select or Create List to Add the imported contacts during this step to keep them organized.
