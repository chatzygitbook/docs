The **Lists** page is where you can create and manage groups of your contacts. Organizing your contacts into lists allows you to target specific audiences for your campaigns and journeys.

## Creating a List

To create a new list, click the **`+ Create`** button in the top right corner. A form will appear where you can give your list a **Name** and a **Description**.

Once created, all lists will be visible on the dashboard. The table columns provide a quick overview of each list:

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | The custom name you gave the list. |
| **Type** | The type of list, indicating how it is managed. |
| **Description** | A brief explanation of the list's purpose. |
| **Members** | The total number of contacts in the list. |
| **Created at** | The date and time the list was created. |
| **Updated at** | The date and time the list was last updated. |

---

## Adding Contacts to a List

Once you create a list:  

1. Navigate back to the **Contacts Dashboard**.  
2. Select the contacts you want to add.  
3. At the bottom-left corner, click the **Add to List** option.  
4. Choose your preferred list and confirm.  

Your selected contacts will now be part of the chosen list.
