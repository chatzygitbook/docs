A **Journey** defines a structured flow to automate and manage user or customer interactions across multiple touchpoints.

---

#### Structure

A Journey consists of a predefined sequence of steps, including:
- Sending messages  
- Waiting for user actions  
- Making decisions  
- Triggering events  

---

#### Purpose

The primary goal is to guide users through a process in a **consistent** and **personalized** manner.

---

#### Automation Features


- Assign AI Agent to an incoming conversation on a particular channel
- Transferring conversation from one AI Agent to another
- Automated customer journeys - Sending lead nurture
- Sending qualified lead to an external CRM
- Connecting Chatzy AI with external system

---

#### Benefits

- Ensures **timely and relevant interactions**  
- **Reduces manual work** and improves efficiency
