A **Journey** defines a structured flow to automate and manage user or customer interactions across multiple touchpoints. It helps organizations streamline conversations, reduce manual effort, and ensure every user receives consistent and personalized experiences.  

---

#### Purpose

The primary goal of a Journey is to **guide users through a process** in a way that feels natural yet controlled.  
- It ensures the experience is **consistent** so every user follows a reliable path.  
- It adapts the flow to be **personalized** based on user input, history, or attributes.  
- Journeys can cover a wide range of scenarios, from simple FAQ resolution to complex multi-step lead qualification and onboarding.  

---

#### Automation Features

Journeys come with powerful automation capabilities that allow businesses to scale without the human touch:  

- **Assign AI Agent to a conversation**: Automatically route incoming chats on a specific channel to a designated AI Agent.  
- **Seamless Agent Handoffs**: Transfer the conversation from one AI agent to another when needed.  
- **Automated customer journeys**: Run lead nurturing campaigns, or reminders without manual intervention.  
- **CRM Integration**: Automatically push qualified leads into external CRMs, ensuring sales teams get timely updates.  
- **System Connectivity**: Connect Chatzy AI with external systems and APIs to fetch or send data in real time.  

---

#### Benefits

By setting up automated Journeys, you gain both operational and customer experience advantages:  

- **Timely and relevant interactions**: Users receive answers, updates, or follow-ups exactly when they need them.  
- **Reduced manual work**: Repetitive tasks are automated, freeing up agents for high-value interactions.  
- **Improved efficiency**: Faster resolution times and streamlined workflows across teams.  
- **Personalization at scale**: Every user feels like they’re getting tailored support, even when interacting with automation.  
- **Consistency across channels**: No matter where the user starts WhatsApp, website, or app the experience remains uniform.
