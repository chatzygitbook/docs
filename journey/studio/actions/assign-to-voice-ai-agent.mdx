---
title: "Assign to Voice AI Agent"
---

The **Assign to Voice AI Agent** action routes the incoming voice call to a designated voice AI agent. This is particularly useful for automating call handling.

<Frame>
  <img src="/journey/studio/actions/assign-to-voice-ai-agent.png" alt="Assign to Voice AI Agent" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

### Configuration Field

- **AI Agent**  
  Dropdown menu to select the specific AI agent that will handle the call.
