---
title: "Transfer to AI Agent"

---
The **Transfer to AI Agent** action hands off the current conversation to another AI agent. Unlike the **Assign to AI Agent** action, this is typically used to move a conversation to an agent with a specific skillset or a different knowledge base.

<Frame>
  <img src="/journey/studio/actions/transfer-to-ai-agent.png" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
#### Configuration Field

- **AI Agent**  
  Dropdown to select the agent that will permanently take over the conversation. Agents are categorized as:  
  - **Conversational AI Agents**: General-purpose bots for free-form conversation and information retrieval.  
  - **Flow-Based AI Agents**: Journey-based bots built in the Studio, designed to follow structured, predefined flows.
