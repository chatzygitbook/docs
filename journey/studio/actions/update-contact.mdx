

The **Update Contact** action adds or modifies details on a contact's profile. It is a key feature for enriching customer data, enabling segmentation, and supporting personalization in future conversations.

#### Configuration Fields

- **Contact Phone Number**  
  The unique identifier for the contact whose profile is being updated.

- **Tags**  
  Labels that categorize a contact. For example, you might add a **Qualified** tag after a user completes a specific step in your flow.

- **Attributes**  
  Custom data fields used to store structured details such as name, email, or company. Attributes differ from tags in that they hold actual values instead of simple categories.
