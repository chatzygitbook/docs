---
title: "Reply to Instagram Comments"

---

The **Reply to Instagram Comments** action automatically responds to comments on your Instagram posts. <br/>
You can add one or more reply messages, and the system will randomly choose one for each new comment. <br/>
 This helps create more natural interactions while reducing repetitive responses.
