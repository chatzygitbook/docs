---
title: "Transfer to Human Agent"

---
The **Transfer to Human Agent** action hands off the current conversation to Human agent. Unlike the **Assign to Agent** action, this is typically used to move a conversation to an agent with a specific skillset or a different knowledge base.

<Frame>
  <img src="/journey/studio/actions/transfer-to-human-agent.png" alt="Transfer to Human Agent step placed in a journey" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

## Configuration Field

- No Config needed


You must add this action in **Inbound Webhook** trigger.
