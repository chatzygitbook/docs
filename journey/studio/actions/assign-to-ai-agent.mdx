The **Assign to AI Agent** action Assigns the conversation to a AI agent. This is useful for building modular, specialized bots that handle different parts of the customer journey.

## Configuration Field

- **AI Agent**  
  Dropdown to select the specific agent to continue the conversation. Agents are categorized as:  
  - **Conversational AI Agents**: General-purpose bots for free-form conversation and information retrieval.  
  - **Flow-Based AI Agents**: Journey-based bots built in the Studio, designed to follow structured, predefined flows.
