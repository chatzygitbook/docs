---
title: "Assign to Human Agent"

---

The **Assign to Human Agent** action Assigns the conversation to a Human agent. This is useful when you want to handle specific parts of the customer journey.

<Frame>
  <img src="/journey/studio/actions/assign-to-human-agent.png" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>


-#### Configuration Field

- No Config needed


You must add this action in **Conversation created** trigger.
