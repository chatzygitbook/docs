
The **Flow Builder** is where you visually design and organize your conversational journeys.  
The top bar provides several tools to help you manage your workspace and control the publishing process.

#### Top Bar Tools

- **Back to All Journeys** → Returns you to the **Journeys Dashboard** to view and manage all flows.  
- **Fit View** → Adjusts the canvas so all nodes and triggers are visible within the screen.  
- **Zoom** → Zoom in or out for a closer look at specific areas or to see the entire flow.  
- **Toggle Interactivity** → Enables or disables interaction with nodes; useful for reviewing complex flows without editing them.  
- **Align** → Automatically arranges and aligns all nodes for a neat and structured layout.  
- **Publish** → Makes the journey live and active. Once published, the flow will respond to its configured triggers.  

---

#### Adding a Trigger

At the center of the canvas, the **`+ Add Trigger`** button is the starting point of every journey.  
A trigger defines the event that initiates the flow .
