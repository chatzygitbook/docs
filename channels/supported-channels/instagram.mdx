## Setting up Instagram Messaging

The **Instagram Messaging** channel allows you to connect your **Instagram Professional account** (Business or Creator) with Chatzy. Once connected, your AI agent can automatically respond to Instagram Direct Messages, engage followers, capture leads, and provide customer support.

### Prerequisites

Before connecting Instagram Messaging, make sure you have:

1. **An Instagram Professional Account**
   - Your account must be set up as either a **Business** or **Creator** account.

2. **Login Access**
   - Ensure you have access to the Instagram account you want to connect.

3. **Instagram Messaging Enabled**
   - Messaging must be enabled for your Instagram account so Chatzy can send and receive Direct Messages.

---

### Connecting Your Account

Click **Connect Instagram Messaging** and sign in to your Instagram account when prompted.

Instagram will then ask you to authorize Chatzy AI and grant the required permissions. Make sure the following permissions are enabled:

- **View profile and access media**
- **Access and manage comments**
- **Access and manage messages**

<Frame>
  <img src="/channels/supported-channels/instagram-authorization.png" alt="Instagram authorization" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

Click **Allow** to authorize the connection. Once authorization is complete, your Instagram account will be connected to Chatzy.

---

### Key Features

- **Instagram DM Automation** - Automatically respond to Instagram Direct Messages using your AI agent, ensuring customers receive instant replies 24/7.

- **Instagram Comment Automation** - Automatically reply to comments on your Instagram posts or reels and guide users into Direct Messages for personalized conversations and lead generation.

- **Automated Replies** - Instantly answer frequently asked questions, product inquiries, and customer support requests.

- **Human Handoff** - Seamlessly transfer conversations to a human agent whenever manual assistance is required.

---

### Common Use Cases

- **E-commerce** - Handle product inquiries, order updates, and recommendations.
- **Service Businesses** - Book appointments and answer customer questions.
- **Creators & Influencers** - Manage fan interactions, collaborations, and common FAQs.
- **Marketing Campaigns** - Engage users responding to Instagram ads or organic content.
