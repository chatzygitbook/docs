## Setting up Instagram

The **Instagram Messaging** channel allows you to integrate your AI chatbot with your Instagram Business account. This enables your bot to **automate replies**, **engage followers**, and provide **real-time support** directly within Instagram DMs.

#### Prerequisites

Before connecting, make sure you meet the following requirements:

1. **Instagram Business Account**  
   - Your Instagram profile must be set as a Business account to unlock messaging automation features.

2. **Linked Facebook Page with Admin Access**  
   - Your Instagram Business account must be linked to a Facebook Page.  
   - You must have **admin access** to that Page to complete the integration.

3. **Messaging Permissions Enabled**  
   - Ensure that messaging permissions are turned on in your Instagram settings.  
   - This allows the chatbot to send and receive direct messages.

---


#### Key Functions of Instagram Messaging Integration

- **Automated Replies** → Instantly respond to FAQs, product queries, or service inquiries.  
- **Engagement Boost** → Keep followers engaged by handling DMs 24/7.  
- **Lead Capture** → Collect user details (name, email, phone) during chat flows.  
- **Seamless Handoff** → Escalate chats to a human agent when needed.  

---

#### Example Use Cases

- **E-commerce Brands** → Reply to order status queries, recommend products, and share promotions.  
- **Service Providers** → Book appointments or answer client questions.  
- **Content Creators** → Engage with fans, manage collaboration requests, and handle FAQs automatically.
