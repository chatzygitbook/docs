
## Setting up Website
The **Website** channel allows you to embed your AI agent directly onto your website. By adding a simple code snippet, you can provide **real-time support**, **lead capture**, and **customer engagement** to your site visitors without requiring them to leave the page.

#### How to deploy Chatbot to the Website

1. **Select Your AI Agent**  
   From the dropdown menu, choose the AI agent you want to embed on your website. 
2. **Enter Your Website URL**  
   In the input field, enter the URL of the website where you want the chatbot to appear. This helps in generating the correct embed code.

3. **Generate Embed Code**  
   Click **“Generate Embed Code”**. You’ll receive two options for integrating the chatbot into your site.

4. **Add the Code to Your Site**
   - **Embed Code (Floating Bubble)**  
     Copy the provided `<link>` and `<script>` tags and paste them into the `<head>` section of your website’s HTML file.  
     This will display a **floating chat bubble** at the bottom-right corner of your site, allowing users to open and chat anytime.

   - **Iframe Embed (Full Frame)**  
     Copy the provided `<iframe>` code and paste it into the `<body>` of your HTML file where you want the chatbot to appear.  
     This option allows you to embed the chatbot as a **full-size interactive frame** within a page, useful for dedicated support, contact pages, or Mobile app.

---

#### Key Functions of Website Integration

- **Seamless User Experience** → Visitors can interact with your AI agent without leaving the site.  
- **24/7 Support** → Provide round-the-clock answers and assistance.  
- **Lead Capture** → Collect user details directly through chat forms.  
- **Customizable Display** → Choose between a floating chat bubble or a fully embedded iframe.  

---

#### Example Use Cases

- **E-commerce Store** → Offer product recommendations and checkout assistance.  
- **Real Estate Site** → Qualify leads by asking about location, budget, and property type.  
- **Service Business** → Answer FAQs and let users book appointments directly.
