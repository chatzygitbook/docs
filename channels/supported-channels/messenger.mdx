## Setting up Facebook Messenger

The **Facebook Messenger** channel allows you to connect your chatbot to a **Facebook Page**, enabling automated messaging, customer support, and real-time engagement directly within Messenger.

---

### Prerequisites

Before connecting, ensure you meet the following requirements:

1. **Facebook Profile**  
   - You must have a personal Facebook profile to start the integration process.  

2. **Facebook Page with Admin Access**  
   - Your chatbot will link to a specific Facebook Page.  
   - You need to have a Page created and **admin rights** to manage it.  

---

### Permissions Required

During the connection process, the platform will request these permissions:

- **Access to Page Conversations** → Allows the chatbot to read incoming messages and respond automatically.  
- **Access to Your List of Pages** → Displays all Facebook Pages you manage so you can select the correct one for integration.  

---


### Key Functions of Messenger Integration

- **Instant Automated Replies** → Respond to FAQs, product queries, and service requests automatically.  
- **Lead Generation** → Collect customer details such as name, email, and phone directly in Messenger.  
- **24/7 Support** → Provide continuous assistance to customers, even outside business hours.  
- **Seamless Handoff** → Escalate chats to a live agent when human support is required.  

---

### Example Use Cases

- **Retail & E-commerce** → Share product catalogs, handle order queries, and track deliveries.  
- **Customer Support** → Provide automated answers to common questions and escalate complex issues.  
- **Event & Services** → Book tickets, schedule appointments, or send reminders through Messenger.
