## Why Multi-Channel Matters

Deploying your chatbot on multiple platforms ensures:  
- **Broader Reach** → Meet customers where they already are  
- **Consistent Experience** → Provide unified answers across platforms  
- **Higher Engagement** → Increase response rates on preferred channels
