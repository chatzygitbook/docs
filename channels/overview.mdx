The **Channels** page is where you deploy your chatbot across different platforms. By publishing your AI agent on multiple channels, you can reach users wherever they prefer to communicate  from messaging apps to your own website.

---

#### Available Channels

* **WhatsApp**  
  Connect your chatbot to **WhatsApp** to engage with customers directly through the world’s most widely used messaging app. Ideal for customer support, Sales, Campaigns and Broadcast.  
  > Example: A brand runs a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on Instagram, where an AI agent takes over the conversation to chat with users and qualify leads.

* **Facebook Messenger**  
  Deploy your chatbot on **Facebook Messenger** to interact with users visiting your Facebook business page. Great for handling FAQs, appointment bookings, or lead capture.  
  > Example: A salon uses Messenger to schedule and confirm appointments automatically.  

* **Instagram Messaging**  
  Integrate with **Instagram Messaging** to automate responses in DMs, engage followers, and handle customer support right inside Instagram.  
  > Example: An e-commerce brand answers product inquiries instantly when users DM from product posts.  

* **Website**  
  Add your chatbot to your **website** for real-time visitor engagement. This provides instant support, captures leads, and improves conversions.  
  > Example: A real estate site embeds a chatbot to qualify leads by asking about budget, location, and property type.  

---

#### Why Multi-Channel Matters

Deploying your chatbot on multiple platforms ensures:  
- **Broader Reach** → Meet customers where they already are.  
- **Consistent Experience** → Provide unified answers across platforms.  
- **Higher Engagement** → Increase response rates on preferred channels.
