This guide explains how to verify your META Business Portfolio step-by-step using META Business Settings.

Verification helps you:
- Build trust with META
- Access advanced advertising features
- Reduce account restrictions
- Improve ad account credibility
- Display business name instead of number

---

## Step 1: Open META Business Settings

Go to your META Business Portfolio settings dashboard.

https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings/business_info

## Step 1: Add Business Details



Before starting verification, make sure your business information is properly filled.

  <img src="/meta/meta1.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

Required details:
- Legal business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website

Please make sure to include the company’s registered name and registered address in the website footer.

e.g.
<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta2.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>


### Instructions
1. Click **Edit**
2. Add all business information
3. Save the changes

### Important
Your business information should exactly match your official documents.

## Step 4: Start Verification

Here you'll get two options

   <Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta6.png" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>


### Instructions
1. Select Business portfolio
2. Click **Start verification**

---

## Step 5: Begin the Verification Process

META will show a verification introduction popup.


<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta7.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

 Click **Get started**

### Note
META may ask for:
- Government-issued ID
- Business registration details
- Contact verification
- Income Tax Return

---

## Step 6: Choose Verification Type

You will now see two verification options.

### Option 1: Verify an organisation
Choose this if:
- You own a registered company
- You have business documents

### Option 2: Verify yourself
Choose this if:
- You are an individual advertiser
- You do not own a registered business

<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta8.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

#### Recommended
For businesses, select:
- **Verify an organisation**

Then click **Next**.

---

## Step 7: Enter Business Information

Fill all required details carefully.

Required fields:
- Legal business name
- Full address
- City with postcode
- Phone number
- Website

Optional but recommended:
- Business Tax ID
- Registration Number

<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta10.png" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
### Important
- Your website should be active
- Your business’s registered name and registered address in the website footer.
- Details should match official records

---

## Step 8: Select Matching Business Record

META will search public records based on your information.
<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta10.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>

### Instructions
1. Select the correct business listing
2. Verify address and phone number
3. Click **Next**

### If your business is not listed
Click:
- **My business isn't listed**

---

## Step 9: Verify Contact Method

META will ask you to confirm ownership using a verification code.

Available methods:
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- Phone call


<Frame>
  <img src="/meta/meta11.jpeg" alt="wa1" className="rounded-lg" />
</Frame>
### Instructions
1. Choose a verification method
2. Receive the code
3. Enter the code
4. Submit verification

---

## Step 10: Wait for META Review

After submission:
- META reviews your details
- Verification may take a few hours to several days

META may request additional documents if needed.

Possible documents:
- GST Certificate
- Business Registration Certificate
- Utility Bill
- Business PAN
- Domain ownership proof

---

## Common Reasons for Verification Rejection

### Mismatched Business Name
Your submitted business name does not match official records.

### Website Issues
Your website may:
- Be inactive
- Not contain business information
- Not match submitted details

### Incorrect Phone Number
META could not verify your contact number.

### Missing Public Records
Your business may not appear in public databases.

---

## Recommended Best Practices

Before applying for verification:
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication
- Use a domain email address
- Keep all business details consistent
- Use official business documents only
