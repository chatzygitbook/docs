The **Playground** lets you test and validate your AI agent before publishing it to production. It provides a safe environment to verify prompts, responses, tools, and configurations in real time.

---

## Version Selection

The **Version** dropdown allows you to choose which version of your AI agent you want to test.

You can switch between:

- **Live** – Tests the currently published version of your AI agent.
- **Draft Versions** – Tests any saved draft without affecting the live experience.

This makes it easy to compare changes, validate new prompts, and experiment with configurations before publishing them.

---

## Features

- **Test Conversations**  
  Simulate real user interactions and verify how your AI agent responds.

- **Version Testing**  
  Instantly switch between the Live version and any saved Draft to compare behavior.

- **Reset Chat**  
  Clear the current conversation and start a fresh test session at any time.

- **Open Iframe**  
  Launch the chatbot in a separate browser window to test the embedded widget experience.

  > **Tip:** You can share the generated iframe link with your team or clients, allowing them to test the AI agent without requiring access to your Chatzy workspace.

- **File & Voice Testing**  
  Test file uploads and voice input if these capabilities are enabled in the Chat Widget settings.

---

> **Tip:** Always test new drafts in the Playground before publishing them. This helps verify prompts, integrations, tools, and overall conversation flow without impacting your live users.
