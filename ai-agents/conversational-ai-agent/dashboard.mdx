The **Dashboard** section helps you review and analyze all user interactions with your AI Agents.  

You can:  
- Access **Chat Logs** to see conversations between users and your agent.  
- Switch to the **Documents** tab to check uploaded documents by users.  
- Use **Revise Answer** when the bot gives an unsatisfactory response. You can correct it with the expected answer, training the AI Agent to perform better in similar cases.  
- Click the **View Source (Book Icon)** when a response comes from the knowledge base to see the exact document or data used. Helpful for verification and debugging.  
- Filter conversations by date range, interaction type, or show only leads.  
- Export data in a single click for reporting and analysis.  
- Enable **Notifications** to stay updated on new interactions.  
- Control live conversations — click the **power button** in the User Info section to close the chat.  
- Save records with the **Download** button to export logs.  

This makes it easy to monitor performance, check lead quality, verify responses, and continuously optimize your AI Agent.
