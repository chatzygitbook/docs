## Ways Calls Reach Your Agent

| Route | Direction | Plan | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phone number** | Inbound | Any | A 24/7 support or enquiry line |
| **Journeys → [Assign to Voice AI Agent](/journey/studio/actions/assign-to-voice-ai-agent)** | Outbound / Incoming call | Any plan with Journeys | Calling a lead right after a form fill, reminders, follow-ups |
| **Voice broadcast campaigns** | Outbound | Starter and above | Calling a whole list or segment at once |
| **[WhatsApp calling](/how/to-setup-whatsapp-calling)** | Inbound & outbound | Growth and above | Voice calls inside WhatsApp, handled by AI or a human |

> 💡 **Tip:** Every route uses the same published agent, so one well-tuned prompt serves inbound, outbound and WhatsApp calls. Transfers, Journeys and Cal.com need **Standard** mode.

---

## Best Practices for Outbound Calls

* **Call fast** - trigger the call within minutes of the form fill or enquiry, while the lead still remembers you.
* **Say who and why first** - the first sentence should name your business and the reason for the call.
* **Keep it short** - set a short **Call Termination** limit in [Telephony](/ai-agents/voice-ai-agent/settings/telephony) and ask only questions you will act on.
* **Hand off the details** - send long information (links, price lists) as a WhatsApp message through a Journey instead of reading it out.

## Best Practices for Inbound Calls

* **Answer the top questions from Data** - add your 20 most common questions as **FAQs** in the [Data](/ai-agents/voice-ai-agent/data) tab.
* **Allow thinking time** - give the silence timeout a few extra seconds for callers looking up order IDs or dates.
* **Always offer a person** - turn on **Transfer calls to a human** and tell the prompt when to use it (billing, complaints, explicit request).
