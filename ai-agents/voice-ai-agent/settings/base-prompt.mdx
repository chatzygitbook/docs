### The Base Prompt Tab (Voice AI Agent)

The **Base Prompt** tab defines the **core identity**, **conversation style**, and **overall purpose** of your voice-enabled AI agent.  
This is where you shape how your agent *speaks, behaves,* and *guides conversations* during real-time voice interactions.

---

#### **Introductory Message**
This is the **first message** your caller hears when the conversation starts.  
It typically serves as a greeting or an initial statement of purpose - setting the tone for the call.

- You can **toggle this message On or Off** depending on whether you want a custom greeting or a system-generated one.
- Example:  
  > “Hi! You’re speaking with ChatzyAI’s Voice Assistant. How can I help you today?”

---

#### **Base Prompt**
The Base Prompt is the **core instruction** that guides how your AI agent thinks, responds, and maintains consistency throughout the call.

It defines:
- The **agent’s behavior** and level of formality  
- Its **goal or mission** (support, sales, booking, feedback, etc.)  
- The **interaction tone**, ensuring a natural voice experience

Example prompt:  
> “Act as a versatile voice assistant capable of handling customer queries, providing helpful information, and offering personalized support based on user needs. Your goal is to ensure satisfaction and achieve the desired outcome - such as lead generation, issue resolution, or product recommendation.”

---

💡 **Tip:**  
A well-defined Base Prompt ensures your voice agent sounds natural, stays on topic, and delivers consistent results across all user interactions.
