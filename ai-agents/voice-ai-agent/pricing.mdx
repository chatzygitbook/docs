## What You Pay For

* **Voice AI minutes** - Billed per minute of call time. The per-minute rate drops the more minutes you buy at once.
* **Phone number rental** - **₹500/month** per number bought through Chatzy. Numbers connected from Twilio or Contaque are billed by that provider.
* **Platform plan** *(optional for voice)* - Needed for **Voice broadcast campaigns** (Starter, ₹3,499/month) and **WhatsApp calling** (Growth, ₹7,499/month). Prices exclude taxes.

> 💡 **Free to try:** The Free plan includes **10 Voice AI minutes**. Playground web chat and web calls are free; a test call to your phone on Chatzy's number is charged per minute (shown under **Get call from agent**).

## Ways to Spend Fewer Minutes

* Set **Call Termination** close to your longest successful call ([Telephony](/ai-agents/voice-ai-agent/settings/telephony)).
* Keep **Hangup calls on user silence** on, so dead lines end quickly.
* Keep **End of Call** on, so the agent closes as soon as the job is done.
* If you call regularly, prepay for 3, 6 or 12 months to lower the rate.
* Keep calls focused — see [Going Live](/ai-agents/voice-ai-agent/going-live#best-practices-for-outbound-calls).

> ⚙️ **Best Practice:** Review **Minutes billed** in the [Dashboard](/ai-agents/voice-ai-agent/dashboard) every week to spot calls that run long.
