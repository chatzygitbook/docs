#### **What You Can Do**
- **Review complete conversations** — See how users interact with your agents in real time, including every message exchanged.  
-  **Identify active agents** — Instantly know which agent handled each response for better accountability and team coordination.  
- **Revise or improve responses** — Use the **Revise Answer** feature to correct or enhance replies, improving future AI accuracy.  
- **Filter intelligently** — Narrow results by **date range**, **user**, or **interaction type** for focused conversation analysis.  


---

> 💡 **Tip:** Regularly reviewing and revising agent responses helps your system learn faster and perform more naturally over time.
