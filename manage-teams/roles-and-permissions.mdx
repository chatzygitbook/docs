## Team roles and what each one can do

Chatzy AI has five team roles: Super Admin, Admin, Editor, Viewer and Agent.
You assign a role to each team member when you invite them, and that role
decides what they can see and change.

| Role        | Permissions                                                                 |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Super Admin | Full access to everything. Cannot be removed by any other member.           |
| Admin       | Manage everything except removing Super Admins.                             |
| Editor      | Manage bots, conversations, sources, and campaigns. No billing or team settings. |
| Viewer      | Read-only access to dashboards, campaigns, and contacts. No edits allowed.  |
| Agent       | Can only access and respond to conversations. No access to other data or settings. |

### Checking your access

- You can see your role for each team in the **My Teams** section.
- The full list of team members and their roles is visible in **My Team Members**.
- Invitations and their current status are tracked in the **Invitations** tab.

> **Tip:** Click your **profile name** in the bottom-left corner to access your account menu. From there, you can switch between teams you've been added to or log out of your Chatzy account.
