## Overview

The **Invite Team Members** feature allows you to easily add new users to your Chatzy AI account and assign them specific roles with defined permissions.  

---

### Steps to Invite Team Members

* Go to the **Invite Team Members** section in the **bottom left corner** of the sidebar.  
* Click the **Invite** button.  
* Enter the **email address** of the person you want to invite.  
* **Assign a role** to them before sending the invitation.  

---

### Roles in Chatzy AI

In Chatzy AI, you can assign one of the following roles:  

* **Super Admin** – Automatically assigned to the account owner. Full access to all features and cannot be removed.  
* **Admin** – Full access to all areas except removing the Super Admin.  
* **Editor** – Can manage most areas of the platform but cannot perform sensitive actions like managing the team, billing, disconnecting channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger), or accessing advanced chatbot settings.  
* **Viewer** – Can view dashboards, reports, campaign performance, and contacts but cannot change settings or access sensitive features.  
* **Agent** – Can reply to customers from the **Conversations** tab but cannot access other areas of the platform.  
* **Viewer Agent** – Can both reply to conversations and view dashboards, campaign performance, and journey runs.  

---

### Invitation Status

Once an invitation is sent, you can track its status:

* **Pending** – The invite hasn’t been acted on yet.  
* **Accepted** – The user has joined the team.  
* **Rejected** – The invite was declined.  

Invitations **expire after 24 hours** if not accepted. You can also **resend invitations** if needed.  

---

### After Sending the Invite

* The invited user will receive an **email invitation**.  
* If the invitee does not yet have a Chatzy AI account, they can create one using the **same email address**, or simply click the invitation link.  
* After clicking the link, they will be redirected to the **Chatzy AI app > Invitations tab**, where they can **accept or reject** the invite.  
* Once accepted, their status will change to **Active** in your **My Team Members** list.  

---

### Managing Your Invitations

* If someone else invites **you** to their team, you’ll also find the invitation under the **Invitations** section in your account.  
* You can choose to **accept** or **reject** it.  
* On accepting, the team will appear under **Teams I Am Part Of** in your account.
