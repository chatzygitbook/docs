The **Dashboard** is your central hub for **analytics and performance monitoring**.  
It provides a high-level overview of your AI agents' activity while also allowing you to dive deeper into **detailed statistics** and trends.

#### Key Metrics
The **top section** of the dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of your agent's current performance:  

- **Live Conversations** → Number of conversations that are currently active in real time.  
- **Unread** → Total conversations that have not been opened or reviewed yet.  
- **Conversational AI Agents** → Count of general-purpose AI agents live on your workspace.  
- **Flow-Based AI Agents** → Count of journey-driven (structured flow) AI agents.

#### Insights
The **Insights section** provides deeper visibility into your chatbot’s message flow and performance metrics:  

- **Overall Message Stats** → Tracks overall engagement with numbers like `Active Contacts` and `Total Messages`.  
- **WhatsApp Message Stats** → Breaks down platform-specific messaging activity:  
  - **Sent** → Messages sent from the platform.  
  - **Delivered** → Messages successfully delivered to the recipient's device.  
  - **Read** → Messages opened/read by the recipient.  
  - **Failed** → Messages that were not delivered (e.g., due to network or platform issues).  
- **Message Volume by Channel** → A graphical breakdown of message traffic across all connected channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, etc.).  


---

#### Performance & Details
At the **bottom of the dashboard**, you’ll find advanced analytics that help you monitor effectiveness at a granular level:  

- **AI Agent Performance** → Individual analytics for each agent, showing which bots are most effective.  
- **Campaign Performance Analysis** → Insights into outbound campaign effectiveness, message delivery, and user engagement.  
- **Journey Details** → A detailed table tracking the lifecycle of each conversational journey:  
  - **Journey Name** → The title of the journey flow.  
  - **Started** → Number of times the journey was initiated.  
  - **Waiting** → Journeys currently paused at a "Wait" step.  
  - **Completed** → Journeys successfully finished.  
  - **Completion Rate** → % of journeys completed vs. started.  


---

> 💡 **Tip:** Use the **Message Volume by Channel chart** to identify which platforms your users engage with most, then optimize campaigns and agent strategies accordingly.
