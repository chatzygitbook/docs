**Ask Chatzy AI** is your built-in AI assistant for navigating and understanding the Chatzy platform. Whether you're exploring features, troubleshooting an issue, or looking for implementation guidance, Chatzy AI provides instant answers without leaving the dashboard.

## What You Can Do

Chatzy AI is trained on Chatzy's documentation and product knowledge, allowing you to:

- Learn how different Chatzy features work.
- Get step-by-step guidance for setting up AI Agents, Journeys, Channels, and Integrations.
- Understand platform capabilities and best practices.
- Find relevant documentation without manually searching through the docs.
- Troubleshoot common configuration and implementation questions.

---

## Getting Started

Click **Ask Chatzy AI** from the left sidebar to open the assistant.

Simply type your question in the chat box, such as:

- *How do I create a Conversational AI Agent?*
- *How do I connect WhatsApp?*
- *How can I automate Instagram comments?*
- *How do I trigger a Journey from an AI Agent?*

You can also click one of the suggested prompts to quickly start a conversation.

## Key Features

- **Instant Answers** - Get immediate responses to product and implementation questions.
- **Documentation Assistant** - Quickly find explanations and setup guides without browsing multiple pages.
- **Context-Aware Guidance** - Receive recommendations based on Chatzy features and supported workflows.
- **Always Available** - Access help anytime while working inside the platform.

> **Tip:** Ask Chatzy AI works best for questions about Chatzy features, setup, integrations, and implementation guidance. It's a quick way to discover platform capabilities and resolve common questions while building your automations.
